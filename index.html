Common questions

How do I start an Americano?
Tap New event, pick the format and scoring, add the players (you can paste the list from your group chat), choose the number of courts and tap Start event. FourUp draws fair pairings and rotates courts and rests.

A score is wrong. Can I fix it?
Yes. Open the event, go to Rounds and tap the score. Every score stays editable, also after the event has finished.

How does the Apple Watch scorer work?
Start an event on your iPhone, open FourUp on your Apple Watch and pick your court. Tap the top or bottom half to score, then send the result; it appears in the event on your iPhone.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FourUp. Or in FourUp: Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open FourUp and tap Restore on the subscription screen (or Settings → Restore purchases).

Where is my data?
Your events, players and scores are stored on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
